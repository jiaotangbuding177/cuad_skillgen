## Common Patterns

### 1. Exclusivity
*   **Exclusive Appointment/Distribution:** Grants exclusive rights to sell, distribute, or manufacture in a specific territory or field.
    *   *Example:* "Sierra appoints Envision as its exclusive distributor for the Product in the Territory, and Envision agrees to exclusively purchase the Product from Sierra."
*   **Sole Source Purchasing:** Obligations to purchase all requirements exclusively from the supplier.
    *   *Example:* "The Provider Representatives are required to be dedicated solely to providing services to the Recipient and are prohibited from providing such services to the Provider or any other customer."
*   **Explicit Non-Exclusivity:** Clarifications that the agreement does not restrict parties from working with others.
    *   *Example:* "The contract explicitly states that the agreement is non-exclusive, allowing both parties to enter into similar servicing agreements with other persons without consent."

### 2. Competitive Restriction Exception
*   **Pre-existing Rights:** Exceptions for licenses, agreements, or relationships existing prior to the contract.
    *   *Example:* "The non-compete restriction explicitly excludes any pre-existing licenses held by Theismann at the time of the agreement."
*   **Specific Competitor/Category Carve-outs:** Named competitors or categories excluded from restrictions.
    *   *Example:* "The non-compete restriction explicitly excludes products manufactured or distributed by Lifeway and its affiliates, subsidiaries, and parent companies."
*   **Consent-Based Waivers:** Restrictions lifted if written consent is provided.
    *   *Example:* "The exclusivity granted to the franchisee contains exceptions allowing the franchisor to develop or franchise locations at special sites such as airports... [with consent]."

### 3. Non-Compete
*   **Geographic/Product Limitations:** Restrictions tied to specific territories or product lines.
    *   *Example:* "The Agency agrees not to sell competing professional liability products in Nevada during the term of the agreement..."
*   **Post-Termination Survival:** Prohibitions continuing after the contract ends.
    *   *Example:* "Theismann is restricted from using... his property for the advertisement... of any network... identical or substantially similar to Bizzingo's Network during the term and for one year thereafter."
*   **Activity Prohibitions:** Bans on developing, manufacturing, or endorsing competing products.
    *   *Example:* "The Distributor is prohibited from developing, manufacturing, or selling any equipment or service that is competitive to the products offered by Lucid."

### 4. Non-Disparagement
*   **Mutual Reputation Protection:** Covenants not to make defamatory or negative statements.
    *   *Example:* "Each party covenants not to make defamatory, misleading, or disparaging remarks about the other party..."
*   **Extended Scope:** Includes officers, directors, affiliates, and products.
    *   *Example:* "The ValueAct Group agrees not to disparage the Company, its affiliates, or its current and former officers and directors during the Covered Period."
*   **Brand Usage Restrictions:** Prohibitions on using IP in a way that tarnishes reputation.
    *   *Example:* "The Licensee is restricted from using the Licensed Domain Names in any manner that tarnishes, degrades, disparages, or reflects adversely on the Licensor..."

### 5. No-Solicit of Employees
*   **Hiring/Solicitation Bans:** Prohibitions on inducing employees/contractors to leave.
    *   *Example:* "The agreement prohibits both parties from hiring or engaging the other party's employees or independent contractors during the term of the agreement and for six months following its expiration."
*   **Exceptions for General Recruiting:** Allowances for public job postings or unsolicited applications.
    *   *Example:* "...with an exception for unsolicited applicants or those responding to public advertisements."

### 6. No-Solicit of Customers
*   **Customer Data Restrictions:** Prohibitions on using customer lists/records for solicitation.
    *   *Example:* "Nationwide agrees that no person having access to the Servicing Agent's records may use such records or information to solicit any of the Servicing Agent's customers for any purpose."
*   **Post-Termination Cooling-off:** Restrictions on soliciting former customers for a set period.
    *   *Example:* "The Customer is restricted from working with third-party advertisers or partners introduced by Kubient without consent and compensation during the term and for one year after termination."

### 7. Most Favored Nation (MFN)
*   **Price Parity:** Guaranteeing rates/prices are no higher than those offered to other parties.
    *   *Example:* "TrueLink agrees that the rates charged for Hosting Services will not exceed those charged to any other party."
*   **Amendment Obligations:** Requirement to amend the contract if better terms are offered to third parties.
    *   *Example:* "In case that the price and other substantive terms offered by Party B to such entity are more favorable... the Driver User and Party B shall amend the provisions..."

## Review Checklist

*   **Exclusivity:**
    *   [ ] Is the arrangement exclusive, non-exclusive, or co-exclusive?
    *   [ ] Are there minimum volume/purchase commitments tied to exclusivity?
    *   [ ] Are there carve-outs for existing customers or specific territories?
*   **Competitive Restriction Exception:**
    *   [ ] Are there exceptions for pre-existing business relationships?
    *   [ ] Are specific competitors or product lines excluded from restrictions?
    *   [ ] Is consent required to engage in otherwise restricted activities?
*   **Non-Compete:**
    *   [ ] What is the geographic scope of the restriction?
    *   [ ] What is the specific product/service category restricted?
    *   [ ] Does the restriction survive termination? If so, for how long?
*   **Non-Disparagement:**
    *   [ ] Is the obligation mutual?
    *   [ ] Does it cover affiliates, officers, and directors?
    *   [ ] Does it survive termination?
*   **No-Solicit of Employees:**
    *   [ ] Does the restriction apply to employees, contractors, or both?
    *   [ ] Is there an exception for general public job postings?
    *   [ ] What is the post-termination survival period?
*   **No-Solicit of Customers:**
    *   [ ] Is there a prohibition on using customer data/lists?
    *   [ ] Does the restriction apply to customers introduced by the counterparty?
    *   [ ] What is the post-termination survival period?
*   **Most Favored Nation:**
    *   [ ] Is there a price protection clause?
    *   [ ] Does it apply to new features or additional functionality?
    *   [ ] Is there an obligation to amend the contract if better terms are offered elsewhere?

## Evidence Extraction Rules

1.  **Locate Clauses:** Search for keywords: "exclusive," "non-compete," "solicit," "disparage," "most favored," "competitor," "restrict," "prohibit."
2.  **Extract Scope:** Identify the specific entities (employees, customers, products), geographies, and timeframes (term, post-termination) mentioned in the clause.
3.  **Identify Exceptions:** Look for "provided that," "except," "excluding," or "carve-out" language within the restriction clauses.
4.  **Verify Mutuality:** Check if the obligation applies to one party or both ("each party," "mutual").
5.  **Capture Survival:** Note any specific durations mentioned for post-termination obligations (e.g., "12 months following termination").

## Boundary Rules

*   **Do Not** infer restrictions that are not explicitly stated in the text.
*   **Do Not** confuse "Non-Compete" with "Exclusivity" unless the contract explicitly links them (e.g., exclusivity implies non-compete in that territory).
*   **Do Not** ignore exceptions/carve-outs; they are critical for accurate risk assessment.
*   **Do Not** assume mutuality unless explicitly stated ("each party," "mutual").
*   **Do** distinguish between restrictions during the term and post-termination survival periods.
*   **Do** capture specific geographic and product limitations for Non-Compete clauses.
