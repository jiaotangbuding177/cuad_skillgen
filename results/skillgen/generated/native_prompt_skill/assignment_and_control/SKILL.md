# SKILL.md - Assignment and Control Review

## Overview

This skill enables you to review contracts for provisions related to **Change of Control** and **Anti-Assignment**. You will analyze contract language to determine whether a party has the right to terminate or if consent/notice is required when a party undergoes a change of control (e.g., merger, stock sale, asset transfer) or attempts to assign the contract to a third party.

## Review Steps

### Step 1: Locate Relevant Clauses
Search the contract for sections typically titled:
- "Assignment"
- "Change of Control"
- "Termination"
- "Successors and Assigns"
- "General Provisions"

### Step 2: Analyze Change of Control Provisions
Look for language that addresses what happens when a party undergoes a change of control (merger, acquisition, sale of substantially all assets, etc.).

**What to look for:**
- Does the contract give the other party the right to terminate upon a change of control?
- Is consent or notice required before a change of control can occur?
- Are there exceptions (e.g., mergers with affiliates)?

### Step 3: Analyze Anti-Assignment Provisions
Look for language that restricts or requires consent for assignment of the contract.

**What to look for:**
- Is prior written consent required for assignment?
- Are there exceptions (e.g., assignment to affiliates, successors by merger)?
- Is notice required upon assignment?

### Step 4: Document Findings
Record your analysis for each category with supporting citations from the contract.

## What to Look For in Contracts

### Change of Control Indicators

**Termination Right Language:**
- "Either party may terminate this agreement if there is any change of control..."
- "Upon a change of control, the non-changing party shall have the right to terminate..."
- "In the event of a merger or consolidation, [Party] shall have the right to terminate..."

**Consent/Notice Language:**
- "No change of control shall occur without the prior written consent of [Party]..."
- "Party shall provide written notice to the other party within [X] days of any change of control..."

### Anti-Assignment Indicators

**Consent Required:**
- "Neither party shall assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party..."
- "This Agreement may not be assigned by either party without the prior written consent of the other..."

**Notice Required:**
- "Party shall notify the other party in writing of any assignment of this Agreement..."

**Exceptions to Consent/Notice:**
- "...except to an affiliate"
- "...except to a successor by merger or acquisition"
- "...except upon sale of all or substantially all assets"

## Examples from Provided Contracts

### Example 1: Change of Control - Termination Right

**Contract:** LUCIDINC Distributor Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Lucid has the right to terminate if there is any change of control of the Distributor.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 16(B): "In addition, Lucid may terminate this agreement by giving the Distributor Written Notice if there is any change of control, ownership or management of the Distributor."

**Analysis:** This is a clear change of control termination right granted to Lucid only. The Distributor does not have a reciprocal right.

---

### Example 2: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required with Exceptions

**Contract:** MTI Technology Corporation Reseller Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Prior written consent is required for assignment, with exceptions for McDATA.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 12.5: "Neither party will assign this Agreement or any rights hereunder without the prior written consent of the other party, which consent will not be unreasonably withheld. Notwithstanding the foregoing sentence, McDATA may assign this Agreement to any entity controlled by, controlling, or under common control with McDATA or to any successor by merger, divestiture, consolidation or reorganization, or to any purchasers of all or substantially all of the assets of the business of McDATA without consent of Reseller."

**Analysis:** Both parties need consent to assign, but McDATA has unilateral exceptions for affiliate transfers, mergers, and asset sales. Reseller has no such exceptions.

---

### Example 3: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required, No Exceptions

**Contract:** Joint Venture Agreement (Accelerated Technologies)

**Finding:** Yes - Consent is required for any transfer of interest.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 12: "No Joint Venturer shall be authorized or empowered to mortgage, hypothecate, pledge, sell, or transfer, an interest in the Joint Venture, nor confer on any successor or assignee the right to become a Joint Venturer without the consent of the other Joint Venturer."

**Analysis:** This is a strict anti-assignment provision with no exceptions. Any transfer requires the other joint venturer's consent.

---

### Example 4: Change of Control - Termination Right with Notice Period

**Contract:** Mossimo Inc. Endorsement Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Licensor has the right to terminate upon merger or consolidation of Company.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 20: "In the event of the merger or consolidation of Company with any other entity, Licensor shall have the right to terminate the Contract Period by so notifying Company in writing within sixty (60) days following Licensor's receipt of notice of such merger or consolidation."

**Analysis:** Licensor has a termination right exercisable within 60 days of receiving notice of a merger or consolidation. Company must provide notice of such event.

---

### Example 5: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required with Mutual Exceptions

**Contract:** Columbia Laboratories Manufacturing and Supply Agreement Amendment

**Finding:** Yes - Prior written consent is required, with mutual exceptions for affiliates and successors.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 22 (amending Section 13.2): "Neither party shall, without the prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed) of the other party having been obtained, assign or transfer this Agreement to any person or entity, in whole or in part (and any attempt to do so shall be void), provided that, each party may assign or transfer this Agreement without such consent to any Affiliate or to any successor by merger of such party, or upon a sale or other transfer of all or substantially all of such party's assets or business to which the subject matter of this Agreement pertains..."

**Analysis:** Both parties need consent for assignment, but both have mutual exceptions for affiliate transfers, mergers, and asset sales. The consent standard is "not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed."

---

### Example 6: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required, Limited Exceptions

**Contract:** CCA Industries Outsourcing Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Prior written consent is required, with limited exceptions for Company.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 9.04: "This Agreement may not be assigned by either party without the prior written consent of the other party, except that, without such consent, (i) Company may make an assignment of this Agreement as collateral security in favor of its lenders, and (ii) the Company may assign this Agreement to a purchaser of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company's business related to the Products."

**Analysis:** Both parties need consent for assignment, but Company has two exceptions (collateral assignment and asset sale). Contractor has no exceptions.

---

### Example 7: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required, Mutual Exceptions

**Contract:** Vitalibis Services and Hosting Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Prior written consent is required, with mutual exceptions for asset acquisitions.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 11.6: "Neither Party may assign this Agreement or otherwise transfer in any way any of the rights and obligations arising out of this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. Notwithstanding the foregoing, (1) either Party may assign this Agreement to any entity who acquires (by merger, acquisition, or otherwise) all or substantially all of the business assets of such Party applicable to the subject matter of this Agreement..."

**Analysis:** Both parties need consent for assignment, but both have a mutual exception for asset acquisitions. VOTOCAST also has the right to subcontract without consent.

---

### Example 8: Anti-Assignment - Consent Required, No Exceptions

**Contract:** XIMAGE Maintenance Agreement

**Finding:** Yes - Prior written consent is required, with no exceptions stated.

**Supporting Text:**
> Section 18: "The interests of MORPHO in this Agreement are personal and shall not be assigned, transferred, shared or divided in any manner by MORPHO without a prior written consent of XIMAGE."

**Analysis:** Only MORPHO's interests are restricted from assignment without XIMAGE's consent. XIMAGE is not similarly restricted. No exceptions are provided.

## Common Patterns Observed

1. **Unilateral vs. Mutual Provisions:** Some contracts grant termination or consent rights to only one party (e.g., Lucid can terminate for Distributor's change of control, but not vice versa).

2. **Exceptions for Affiliates and Successors:** Many contracts allow assignment without consent to affiliates or successors by merger/asset sale.

3. **Consent Standards:** When consent is required, it may be subject to "not to be unreasonably withheld" or similar standards.

4. **Notice Requirements:** Some provisions require notice of assignment or change of control even if consent is not required.

5. **Change of Control as Termination Event:** This is often a separate termination right, not just an assignment restriction.
