## Evidence-Based Review Rules

### 1. Rofr/Rofo/Rofn (Right of First Refusal/Offer/Negotiation)
*   **Absence of Rights**: Verify if the contract explicitly states that no rights of first refusal, offer, or negotiation are granted. Look for negative assertions such as "No..." or explicit denials [KA-0001, KA-0014, KA-0021, KA-0032, KA-0062, KA-0085, KA-0090, KA-0114, KA-0130, KA-0139, KA-0155, KA-0174, KA-0189, KA-0201, KA-0206, KA-0249, KA-0254, KA-0264, KA-0285, KA-0290, KA-0300, KA-0312].
*   **Grant of Right of First Refusal**: Identify clauses granting a right of first refusal for specific opportunities, such as participation in promotions or licensing of new works [KA-0218, KA-0317].
*   **Grant of Right of First Negotiation**: Identify clauses granting a right of first negotiation regarding licensing rights for new content or releases [KA-0307].

### 2. IP Ownership Assignment
*   **Retention of Existing IP**: Verify clauses stating that each party retains all rights, title, and interest in their respective pre-existing content and intellectual property [KA-0002, KA-0022, KA-0086, KA-0140, KA-0202, KA-0207, KA-0219].
*   **Assignment of Created IP to Company/Client**: Look for explicit assignments of ownership for work product, web pages, commercials, or proprietary materials created by a contractor or developer to the Company/Client [KA-0019, KA-0081, KA-0099, KA-0115, KA-0126, KA-0127, KA-0128, KA-0144, KA-0145, KA-0146, KA-0147, KA-0160, KA-0172, KA-0175, KA-0215].
*   **No Assignment Clauses**: Identify contracts that do not contain clauses assigning ownership of IP created by one party to the counterparty [KA-0131, KA-0156].

### 3. Joint IP Ownership
*   **Explicit Joint Ownership**: Identify clauses establishing joint ownership of inventions or IP jointly made under the agreement, including mechanisms for assigning undivided interests [KA-0087, KA-0161, KA-0162].
*   **Sole Ownership/No Joint Ownership**: Verify clauses stating that IP remains the sole property of the originating party or Licensor, with no provision for joint ownership [KA-0003, KA-0023, KA-0034, KA-0064, KA-0092, KA-0116, KA-0132, KA-0141, KA-0157, KA-0176, KA-0191, KA-0203, KA-0208, KA-0221, KA-0251, KA-0256, KA-0266, KA-0287, KA-0293, KA-0302, KA-0314, KA-0347].

### 4. Covenant Not to Sue
*   **Restrictions on Contesting Validity**: Identify clauses where a party agrees not to attack, challenge, or contest the validity of the counterparty's intellectual property, trademarks, or title [KA-0222, KA-0223, KA-0323, KA-0356, KA-0357, KA-0363].
*   **Absence of Covenant**: Verify if the contract does not contain a covenant not to sue or restrictions on contesting IP validity [KA-0004, KA-0017, KA-0065, KA-0088, KA-0093, KA-0117, KA-0133, KA-0158, KA-0177, KA-0192, KA-0209, KA-0252, KA-0257, KA-0267, KA-0288, KA-0315, KA-0348, KA-0385, KA-0390].

### 5. Third Party Beneficiary
*   **Exclusion of Third Parties**: Identify clauses explicitly stating that the agreement is solely for the benefit of the parties (and their successors/assigns) and does not confer rights on third parties [KA-0013, KA-0025, KA-0026, KA-0066, KA-0094, KA-0143, KA-0193, KA-0198, KA-0211, KA-0216, KA-0268, KA-0295, KA-0316, KA-0330].
*   **Designation of Third Party Beneficiary**: Identify clauses explicitly designating a non-contracting party (e.g., an Agent) as a beneficiary with enforcement rights [KA-0125].
*   **Absence of Identification**: Verify if the contract does not identify any non-contracting party as a beneficiary [KA-0005, KA-0036, KA-0089, KA-0118, KA-0134, KA-0159, KA-0210, KA-0258, KA-0289, KA-0304].

## Review Checklist

### Rofr/Rofo/Rofn
- [ ] Check for explicit grants of Right of First Refusal (ROFR) [KA-0218, KA-0317].
- [ ] Check for explicit grants of Right of First Negotiation (ROFN) [KA-0307].
- [ ] Confirm absence of ROFR/ROFO/ROFN clauses if not found [KA-0001, KA-0014, KA-0021, KA-0032, KA-0062, KA-0085, KA-0090, KA-0114, KA-0130, KA-0139, KA-0155, KA-0174, KA-0189, KA-0201, KA-0206, KA-0249, KA-0254, KA-0264, KA-0285, KA-0290, KA-0300, KA-0312].

### IP Ownership Assignment
- [ ] Verify retention of background/pre-existing IP by each party [KA-0002, KA-0022, KA-0086, KA-0140, KA-0202, KA-0207, KA-0219].
- [ ] Identify assignment of foreground IP/work product to the Company/Client [KA-0019, KA-0081, KA-0099, KA-0115, KA-0126, KA-0127, KA-0128, KA-0144, KA-0145, KA-0146, KA-0147, KA-0160, KA-0172, KA-0175, KA-0215].
- [ ] Note if no assignment clauses exist [KA-0131, KA-0156].

### Joint IP Ownership
- [ ] Identify any clauses establishing joint ownership of IP [KA-0087, KA-0161, KA-0162].
- [ ] Confirm sole ownership provisions if joint ownership is absent [KA-0003, KA-0023, KA-0034, KA-0064, KA-0092, KA-0116, KA-0132, KA-0141, KA-0157, KA-0176, KA-0191, KA-0203, KA-0208, KA-0221, KA-0251, KA-0256, KA-0266, KA-0287, KA-0293, KA-0302, KA-0314, KA-0347].

### Covenant Not to Sue
- [ ] Check for covenants restricting challenges to IP validity or title [KA-0222, KA-0223, KA-0323, KA-0356, KA-0357, KA-0363].
- [ ] Confirm absence of such covenants if not found [KA-0004, KA-0017, KA-0065, KA-0088, KA-0093, KA-0117, KA-0133, KA-0158, KA-0177, KA-0192, KA-0209, KA-0252, KA-0257, KA-0267, KA-0288, KA-0315, KA-0348, KA-0385, KA-0390].

### Third Party Beneficiary
- [ ] Identify explicit exclusions of third-party beneficiaries [KA-0013, KA-0025, KA-0026, KA-0066, KA-0094, KA-0143, KA-0193, KA-0198, KA-0211, KA-0216, KA-0268, KA-0295, KA-0316, KA-0330].
- [ ] Identify explicit designations of third-party beneficiaries [KA-0125].
- [ ] Note if no third-party beneficiary status is identified [KA-0005, KA-0036, KA-0089, KA-0118, KA-0134, KA-0159, KA-0210, KA-0258, KA-0289, KA-0304].
