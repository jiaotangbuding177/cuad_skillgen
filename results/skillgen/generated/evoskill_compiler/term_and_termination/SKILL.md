# SKILL.md: Term and Termination Review

3. **Termination for Convenience**: Evidence atoms (KA-0005 to KA-0349)

## Evidence-Based Review Rules

### 1. Renewal Term
*   **Identify Automatic Renewal Mechanisms**: Look for language indicating automatic renewal upon expiration of the initial term. Common patterns include "automatically renewed for successive one (1) year periods" [KA-0077, KA-0111, KA-0133, KA-0142, KA-0170, KA-0285, KA-0307, KA-0333, KA-0336, KA-0391, KA-0400, KA-0436] or "renew automatically from year to year" [KA-0016].
*   **Determine Renewal Duration**: Check if the renewal term matches the initial term or differs. Evidence shows variations such as successive one-year terms [KA-0001, KA-0016, KA-0077, KA-0111, KA-0133, KA-0142, KA-0170, KA-0285, KA-0307, KA-0333, KA-0336, KA-0391, KA-0400, KA-0436], two-year terms [KA-0327], three-year terms [KA-0195, KA-0318, KA-0319], five-year terms [KA-0204], or ten-year terms [KA-0259, KA-0375].
*   **Identify Perpetual/Indefinite Terms**: Flag agreements that lack a fixed renewal term and continue indefinitely. Look for phrases like "continue indefinitely unless sooner terminated" [KA-0299] or "enduring in perpetuity" [KA-0464, KA-0465].
*   **Check for Conditional Renewals**: Identify if renewal is subject to specific thresholds or options. Examples include renewal upon meeting a "First Renewal Threshold" [KA-0318] or requiring an explicit option exercise by the Distributor [KA-0195, KA-0259, KA-0375].
*   **Identify Month-to-Month Renewals**: Detect agreements that shift to a month-to-month basis after the initial term, e.g., "continue on a month to month basis" [KA-0164].

### 2. Notice Period to Terminate Renewal
*   **Determine Required Notice Window**: Identify the specific timeframe required to prevent automatic renewal. Common periods include:
    *   30 days: [KA-0002, KA-0078, KA-0112, KA-0143, KA-0165, KA-0482, KA-0512, KA-0041]
    *   60 days: [KA-0017, KA-0334, KA-0337, KA-0476]
    *   90 days: [KA-0196]
    *   6 months (180 days): [KA-0205, KA-0308, KA-0328, KA-0392, KA-0401, KA-0496]
    *   120 days: [KA-0503]
    *   1 year: [KA-0027]
    *   3 months: [KA-0171]
    *   9-12 months: [KA-0376]
*   **Identify Notice Directionality**: Determine if notice is required from either party or only specific parties. Most clauses allow "either party" to provide notice [KA-0002, KA-0017, KA-0078, KA-0112, KA-0143, KA-0165, KA-0171, KA-0205, KA-0308, KA-0328, KA-0334, KA-0337, KA-0392, KA-0401, KA-0476, KA-0496, KA-0503, KA-0512, KA-0041]. Some restrict notice to specific roles, such as the Distributor [KA-0196] or Franchisee [KA-0376].
*   **Check for Automatic Expiration Without Notice**: Identify clauses where the agreement expires automatically without requiring notice, e.g., "expire and terminate automatically without further notice" [KA-0320, KA-0321].

### 3. Termination for Convenience
*   **Identify Existence of Convenience Clause**: Look for explicit language allowing termination "without cause," "for any reason," or "in their sole discretion."
    *   Explicit "without cause": [KA-0005, KA-0039, KA-0114, KA-0115, KA-0186, KA-0226, KA-0287, KA-0329, KA-0348, KA-0349]
    *   "For any reason": [KA-0329]
    *   "Sole discretion": [KA-0005]
*   **Determine Notice Period for Convenience Termination**: If a convenience clause exists, identify the required notice period.
    *   30 days: [KA-0032, KA-0033, KA-0113, KA-0144, KA-0166, KA-0226]
    *   60 days: [KA-0018, KA-0114, KA-0349]
    *   90 days: [KA-0005, KA-0039, KA-0287, KA-0348]
    *   6 months: [KA-0309, KA-0329]
    *   3 months: [KA-0172]
*   **Identify Absence of Convenience Clause**: Flag agreements that *only* allow termination for cause (e.g., material breach, insolvency, performance failure).
    *   No convenience right: [KA-0079, KA-0135, KA-0197, KA-0301, KA-0322, KA-0323, KA-0338]
*   **Check for Conditional Convenience Rights**: Identify if convenience termination is limited to specific periods, such as "prior to delivery of the Product" [KA-0186] or "during a renewed term" [KA-0309].
