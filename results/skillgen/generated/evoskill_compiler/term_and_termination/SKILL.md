## Common Clause Patterns & Example Phrasing

### Category: Renewal Term

#### Pattern 1: Automatic Annual Renewal
- **Description**: The agreement automatically extends for successive one-year periods upon expiration of the initial term unless terminated by notice. This is the most common renewal structure.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "Thereafter, this agreement will renew automatically from year to year unless cancelled in writing by either Party giving the other written notice of such cancellation a minimum of 60 days before the e..." [KA-0016]
  > "shall be automatically renewed for successive one (1) year periods" [KA-0111]
- **Variation Notes**: Some contracts specify "calendar year" [KA-0170] or "month to month" [KA-0164] instead of fixed annual terms.

#### Pattern 2: Multi-Year Automatic Renewal
- **Description**: The agreement automatically renews for terms longer than one year (e.g., 2, 3, or 5 years).
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "Thereafter, the agreement shall automatically renew for successive five (5)-year terms" [KA-0204]
  > "shall thereafter automatically renew for additional two (2) year terms" [KA-0327]
- **Variation Notes**: Often tied to specific milestones or thresholds before the automatic renewal triggers [KA-0318, KA-0475].

#### Pattern 3: Option to Renew (Non-Automatic)
- **Description**: Renewal is not automatic; one or both parties must actively exercise an option to renew, often requiring mutual written agreement.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "This Agreement may be renewed for any number of successive one (1) year terms by mutual written agreement of the parties prior to the conclusion of the term of this Agreement." [KA-0001]
  > "Distributor shall have the option to renew this Agreement for an additional Three (3) year period" [KA-0195]
- **Variation Notes**: May be limited to a specific number of renewal terms [KA-0486] or require compliance with refurbishment/performance standards [KA-0259].

#### Pattern 4: Indefinite/Perpetual Term
- **Description**: The agreement has no fixed end date and continues indefinitely until terminated.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and shall continue indefinitely unless sooner terminated as provided in this Article 5." [KA-0299]
  > "enduring in perpetuity unless terminated as provided for elsewhere in this AGREEMENT" [KA-0464]
- **Variation Notes**: Often found in license agreements or strategic alliances where long-term continuity is expected [KA-0465, KA-0481].

### Category: Notice Period to Terminate Renewal

#### Pattern 1: Short Notice (30 Days)
- **Description**: A party must provide 30 days' written notice prior to the end of the term to prevent automatic renewal.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "A party wishing to renew this Agreement shall give the other party notice thereof no less than thirty (30) days before the expiration of the term then in effect." [KA-0002]
  > "unless written notice is given by either Party no later than thirty (30) days in advance of the expiration of the Initial Term or the applicable Renewal Term." [KA-0512]
- **Variation Notes**: Common in month-to-month or short-term agreements [KA-0165, KA-0078].

#### Pattern 2: Medium Notice (60-90 Days)
- **Description**: A party must provide 60 to 90 days' written notice prior to the end of the term.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "giving the other written notice of such cancellation a minimum of 60 days before the end of the then current term." [KA-0017]
  > "by providing prior written notice to Company within Ninety (90) days of the end of the initial period of this Agreement" [KA-0196]
- **Variation Notes**: 60 days is a standard commercial notice period [KA-0334, KA-0125].

#### Pattern 3: Long Notice (6 Months / 180 Days)
- **Description**: A party must provide at least 6 months (or 180 days) written notice prior to the end of the term.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "unless either Party gives written notice to the other Party of intent not to renew at least six (6) months prior to the expiration of the then-current Term" [KA-0205]
  > "unless a Party provides the other with written notice of termination at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term." [KA-0496]
- **Variation Notes**: Often used in long-term strategic alliances or complex supply agreements [KA-0308, KA-0401].

#### Pattern 4: Extended Notice (1 Year)
- **Description**: A party must provide one year's written notice prior to the end of the term.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "one (1) year written notice prior to the expiration of the initial period or any extension period thereof" [KA-0027]
- **Variation Notes**: Rare, typically reserved for highly specialized or long-duration manufacturing/supply agreements.

### Category: Termination for Convenience

#### Pattern 1: Mutual Termination for Convenience
- **Description**: Either party may terminate the agreement without cause, provided they give specified written notice.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "Each of the Recipient and the Provider may, in their sole discretion, terminate this Agreement in whole or in part, at any time without cause, and without liability except..." [KA-0005]
  > "Either party may, at its option, terminate this Agreement without cause, effective at any time after January 31, 1999, upon giving at least ninety (90) days prior written notice of such termination to..." [KA-0039]
- **Variation Notes**: Notice periods vary (30, 60, 90 days) [KA-0115, KA-0287].

#### Pattern 2: Unilateral Termination for Convenience
- **Description**: Only one specific party (e.g., Client, Licensor, or Agent) has the right to terminate without cause.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "Dragon may terminate this agreement without cause by giving sixty (60) days written notice to MMI." [KA-0114]
  > "The Remarketing Agent may resign and be discharged from its duties and obligations hereunder with respect to the VRRM-MFP Shares by giving 90 days' prior written notice to the Fund and the Calculation..." [KA-0348]
- **Variation Notes**: Often asymmetrical, favoring the paying party or the service provider depending on leverage [KA-0032, KA-0497].

#### Pattern 3: No Termination for Convenience (Cause Only)
- **Description**: The agreement explicitly restricts termination to specific causes (breach, insolvency) and does not allow termination without cause.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "If either party materially breaches any term or condition of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within-thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of the breach, the nonbreaching party m..." [KA-0079]
  > "This Agreement may be terminated by MusclePharm only: (i) In the event Endorser is convicted of a felony. (ii) In the event Endorser is in material breach or default of this Agreement..." [KA-0322]
- **Variation Notes**: Look for language like "only upon," "solely for cause," or absence of "without cause" language [KA-0135, KA-0338].

#### Pattern 4: Termination for Convenience During Renewal Only
- **Description**: Termination for convenience is only permitted during renewal terms or after a specific initial period.
- **Example Phrasing**:
  > "At any time during a renewed term, this Agreement may be terminated by any party on six (6) months prior notice that it wishes to terminate the Agreement." [KA-0309]
  > "After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a month to month basis until terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other setting forth the effective dat..." [KA-0166]
- **Variation Notes**: Protects the initial term stability while allowing exit flexibility later [KA-0113].

## Review Checklist

### Renewal Term
- [ ] **Identify Renewal Mechanism**: Does the contract auto-renew [KA-0016, KA-0111] or require active option exercise [KA-0001, KA-0195]?
- [ ] **Determine Renewal Duration**: Is the renewal term 1 year [KA-0001], multi-year [KA-0204], or indefinite [KA-0299]?
- [ ] **Check for Milestones**: Are there performance thresholds required for automatic renewal [KA-0318, KA-0475]?

### Notice Period to Terminate Renewal
- [ ] **Verify Notice Window**: What is the required notice period to prevent renewal? (30 days [KA-0002], 60 days [KA-0017], 6 months [KA-0205], or 1 year [KA-0027]?)
- [ ] **Confirm Notice Recipient**: Is notice required to be in writing and sent to a specific party [KA-0002, KA-0017]?
- [ ] **Check for Asymmetry**: Do both parties have the same notice period, or is it different for each [KA-0196]?

### Termination for Convenience
- [ ] **Assess Right to Terminate Without Cause**: Does the contract allow termination for convenience [KA-0005, KA-0039] or only for cause [KA-0079, KA-0322]?
- [ ] **Identify Notice Period for Convenience**: If allowed, what is the notice period (30 days [KA-0032], 90 days [KA-0039], 6 months [KA-0309])?
- [ ] **Check for Asymmetry**: Is the right to terminate for convenience mutual [KA-0005] or unilateral [KA-0114, KA-0348]?
- [ ] **Verify Timing Restrictions**: Is termination for convenience allowed at any time [KA-0172] or only after a specific date/term [KA-0039, KA-0309]?
