## Common Clause Patterns & Example Phrasing

### 1. Revenue/Profit Sharing

#### Pattern: Fixed Percentage Royalty on Net/Gross Revenue
**Description:** A clause requiring one party to pay a specific percentage of Net or Gross Revenue/Sales to the other party, often defined as a "Royalty" or "Continuing Fee."
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Zynga will pay to WPT ten percent (10%) of the cumulative Net Revenue ... ("Royalty") from the WPT-branded Zynga Poker Tournament Mode..." [KA-0040]
> "You will pay us ... a monthly Continuing Fee equal to five percent (5%) of the Gross Sales derived from the Franchise..." [KA-0117]
**Variation Notes:** May be based on "Net Sales" [KA-0053], "Gross Revenues" [KA-0342], or "Subscriber Revenue" [KA-0364].

#### Pattern: Tiered Royalty Rates Based on Sales Thresholds
**Description:** Royalty percentages that increase or decrease based on cumulative sales volumes or revenue thresholds.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Annual Net Sales of Product for the Indication Annual Royalty Rate Percent Net Sales $0 - $25,000,000 1.75% >$25,000,000 - $50,000,000 2.25% >$50,000,000 2.50%" [KA-0054]
> "Subscribers Royalty Payable as Percentage of Gross Revenue 0 - 5000 6.25% 5001 - 7500 6.75% ..." [KA-0381]
**Variation Notes:** Thresholds may be based on dollar amounts [KA-0054] or unit counts/subscribers [KA-0381].

#### Pattern: Equal or Defined Profit/Loss Sharing
**Description:** Explicit allocation of income, credits, losses, or deductions between parties, often in Joint Ventures or Strategic Alliances.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "All income and credits, and all losses and deductions shall be owned and shared among the Joint Venturers as follows: 50% to Collectible Concepts Group, Inc. 50% to Pivotal Self Service Tech, Inc." [KA-0009]
> "The policy rebate, award, and preferential treatment shall be shared by both Parties. Each party shares 50% of the policy rebate, award, and preferential treatment." [KA-0159]
**Variation Notes:** May explicitly exclude profit sharing to avoid partnership tax status [KA-0164].

#### Pattern: Revenue Share via Exhibit/Appendix Reference
**Description:** The main agreement references an external exhibit for specific revenue share percentages or formulas.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "plan_b shall pay LICENSOR a share of its revenues as set forth in APPENDIX 2 ("REVENUES")." [KA-0247]
> "The revenue share for the Program is stated in Exhibit B." [KA-0327]
**Variation Notes:** Often used when rates vary by content type or region [KA-0248].

#### Pattern: No Revenue Sharing (Royalty-Free/Fixed Price)
**Description:** Explicit statement that no ongoing revenue share exists, often because the license is royalty-free or fully paid via purchase price.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Consideration for the license granted by SONY under this IP Agreement is included in the Purchase Price, and PURCHASER shall have no obligation to make any additional payments to SONY..." [KA-0354]
> "Licensor hereby grants Licensed Users a ... royalty-free license ... to use and display the Licensed Trade Name..." [KA-0426]
**Variation Notes:** May state "No payment or compensation contemplated" [KA-0407].

### 2. Price Restrictions

#### Pattern: Unilateral Right to Set/Change Prices
**Description:** One party retains the sole discretion to set or change prices, often with notice requirements.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "The submission of advertising information to us for our approval will not affect your right to determine the prices at which you sell your products and/or services." [KA-0119]
> "JRVS shall have the right to revise JRVS Price at any time. Price changes shall apply to all purchase orders received after the effective date with the notice..." [KA-0483]
**Variation Notes:** May require notice periods (e.g., 90 days) [KA-0496] or be restricted to specific conditions (e.g., raw material costs) [KA-0266].

#### Pattern: Price Increase Caps and Notice Requirements
**Description:** Restrictions on how much or how often prices can be increased, often capped at a percentage or frequency.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "VOTOCAST expressly reserves the right to change its rates charged hereunder ... but agrees that rates may not increase by more than ten percent..." [KA-0900]
> "Such Prices and Volume Discount Prices shall only be subject to increase once per year ... provided (i) Company provides Distributor with at least Ninety (90) days' notice..." [KA-0361]
**Variation Notes:** Caps may be absolute (e.g., 10%) [KA-0900] or relative to market rates [KA-0100].

#### Pattern: Fixed Retail Price / MSRP Restrictions
**Description:** Restrictions on the price at which products are sold to end-users, often fixing the price or prohibiting discounts below a certain level.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "The Products shall only be sold by XSPA at the retail price of the applicable Product as set forth on Exhibit A (the "Retail Price")." [KA-0929]
> "Reseller agrees not to publish or advertise any price below Manufacturer's Suggested List Price on its web site..." [KA-0613]
**Variation Notes:** May prohibit advertising below MSRP [KA-0613] or fix the exact retail price [KA-0929].

#### Pattern: Price Approval Requirements
**Description:** One party must obtain approval from the other before changing prices or fees.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Party B may negotiate the mode of cooperation with Party A ... provided, however that the pricing of its business shall be approved by Party A..." [KA-0440]
> "any changes to the prices of information fee ... may be implemented formally only after confirmed by Party A." [KA-0441]
**Variation Notes:** May require filing with authorities in addition to partner approval [KA-0440].

#### Pattern: No Price Restrictions
**Description:** Explicit statement that there are no restrictions on pricing, or that the license is royalty-free with no price controls.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Telnet shall, in its sole discretion, set the prices it charges for the Products and Services it sells or distributes..." [KA-0027]
> "The Company may elect to dispose of the Products at any price or for no consideration in its sole discretion..." [KA-0904]
**Variation Notes:** Often found in endorsement agreements [KA-0850] or royalty-free licenses [KA-0427].

### 3. Minimum Commitment

#### Pattern: Minimum Annual/Monthly Guarantee (MAG)
**Description:** A fixed minimum payment obligation regardless of actual sales or usage, often recoupable against royalties.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Zynga will pay WPT three million U.S. dollars ($3,000,000) per year ... (the "Annual Minimum Guarantee")" [KA-0041]
> "The term "Minimum Royalty" means a minimum monthly Royalty Fee payment ... in the following amounts..." [KA-0344]
**Variation Notes:** May be structured as a non-refundable guarantee [KA-0371] or a fixed annual sum [KA-0424].

#### Pattern: Minimum Purchase/Order Commitment
**Description:** Obligation to purchase a minimum quantity or value of goods/services.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Impresse agrees to purchase from VerticalNet Banners and Newsletters for a total price of at least $[*] as set forth below..." [KA-0492]
> "Provide Performance Guarantee Letter to state that Party B will purchase all electricity generated from the project." [KA-0161]
**Variation Notes:** May be quarterly [KA-0493] or total term [KA-0492].

#### Pattern: Minimum Development/Opening Schedule
**Description:** Obligation to open a specific number of locations or complete development milestones by certain dates.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Developer must submit one or more site(s) for approval ... and open to the public the number of El Pollo Loco® Restaurant(s) on such approved sites each calendar year..." [KA-0293]
> "If Developer fails to meet the opening date for any El Pollo Loco® Restaurant ... Developer shall be in default..." [KA-0295]
**Variation Notes:** Failure to meet schedule often constitutes default [KA-0294].

#### Pattern: Minimum Marketing/Advertising Spend
**Description:** Obligation to spend a minimum amount on marketing or advertising.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "MusclePharm shall allocate between $3,000,000 and $5,000,000 in the First Contract Year ... toward the marketing of the Licensed Products." [KA-0632]
> "During sixty (60) day period ... you will spend a minimum of $10,000 on marketing during the Grand Opening Period." [KA-0522]
**Variation Notes:** May be a percentage of gross revenues with a floor [KA-0521].

#### Pattern: No Minimum Commitment
**Description:** Explicit statement that there are no minimum purchase, sales, or usage commitments.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "ConvergTV has no obligation to Producer to exercise any of its rights under this Agreement." [KA-0329]
> "Liquidmetal makes no commitment that it will purchase any particular volume of any Liquidmetal Products from Eutectix." [KA-0589]
**Variation Notes:** May state "No" to minimum order questions [KA-0332].

### 4. Volume Restriction

#### Pattern: Tiered Fees Based on Usage Volume
**Description:** Fees or royalties that change (increase or decrease) based on the volume of usage, subscribers, or sales.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "Annual Net Sales of Product for the Indication Annual Royalty Rate Percent Net Sales $0 - $25,000,000 1.75% >$25,000,000 - $50,000,000 2.25% ..." [KA-0058]
> "License and Hosting Fees are as shown in the table below: # of Monthly Video Views Fee Up to 9,999 SO Between 10.000 — 24,999 $1.000 ..." [KA-0902]
**Variation Notes:** May be based on subscribers [KA-0381], video views [KA-0902], or units sold [KA-0470].

#### Pattern: Overage Charges for Exceeding Thresholds
**Description:** Additional fees charged when usage exceeds a specified baseline or allowance.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "This includes up to 10 G of bandwidth, with overages at $20 for each 1 G beyond 10 in any given month." [KA-0067]
> "Should BNL desire to attach additional communication lines ... BNL shall pay to VIP the additional fees set forth in paragraph 5(F)..." [KA-0106]
**Variation Notes:** May be per-unit overage [KA-0067] or flat fee for additional lines [KA-0106].

#### Pattern: Volume Caps or Supply Limits
**Description:** Restrictions on the maximum volume of products supplied or services provided.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "In no event will the Company be obligated to provide Products to the Agent in excess of one hundred and twenty percent (120%) of any maximum quantity specified in the applicable Business Plan;" [KA-0094]
> "Eutectix agrees to reserve sufficient manufacturing capacity ... provided that Eutectix shall not be required to reserve more than ten percent (10%) of its manufacturing capacity..." [KA-0590]
**Variation Notes:** May cap total purchase price [KA-0020] or fulfillment fees [KA-0032].

#### Pattern: No Volume Restrictions
**Description:** Explicit statement that there are no volume-based fees, caps, or restrictions.
**Example Phrasing:**
> "The contract does not impose any fees, consent requirements, or restrictions based on the volume of products purchased or sold by the Distributor." [KA-0333]
> "The license is royalty-free and does not contain any volume-based fees, thresholds, or restrictions on the amount of use." [KA-0429]
**Variation Notes:** May state "No" to volume restriction questions [KA-0581].

## Review Checklist

### Revenue/Profit Sharing
- [ ] **Identify Royalty Base:** Does the contract specify Net Revenue, Gross Revenue, or Net Sales? [KA-0040, KA-0117, KA-0053]
- [ ] **Check for Tiered Rates:** Are there different royalty percentages based on sales thresholds? [KA-0054, KA-0381]
- [ ] **Verify Profit Sharing:** Is there an explicit sharing of profits/losses, or is it excluded? [KA-0009, KA-0164]
- [ ] **Locate Exhibit References:** Are revenue share details deferred to an Exhibit or Appendix? [KA-0247, KA-0327]
- [ ] **Confirm No Sharing:** Is the license explicitly royalty-free or fully paid? [KA-0354, KA-0426]

### Price Restrictions
- [ ] **Assess Pricing Discretion:** Who has the right to set or change prices? [KA-0119, KA-0483]
- [ ] **Check for Increase Caps:** Are there limits on price increases (e.g., 10% cap, annual only)? [KA-0900, KA-0361]
- [ ] **Verify Retail Price Controls:** Are there restrictions on end-user pricing (MSRP, fixed price)? [KA-0929, KA-0613]
- [ ] **Identify Approval Requirements:** Must price changes be approved by the other party? [KA-0440, KA-0441]
- [ ] **Confirm No Restrictions:** Is there an explicit statement of no price restrictions? [KA-0027, KA-0904]

### Minimum Commitment
- [ ] **Identify Minimum Guarantees:** Are there minimum annual/monthly payments (MAG)? [KA-0041, KA-0344]
- [ ] **Check Purchase Commitments:** Are there minimum order quantities or values? [KA-0492, KA-0161]
- [ ] **Verify Development Schedules:** Are there minimum opening/development milestones? [KA-0293, KA-0295]
- [ ] **Assess Marketing Spend:** Are there minimum marketing/advertising budgets? [KA-0632, KA-0522]
- [ ] **Confirm No Commitment:** Is there an explicit statement of no minimum commitments? [KA-0329, KA-0589]

### Volume Restriction
- [ ] **Identify Tiered Usage Fees:** Do fees change based on usage volume (subscribers, views, units)? [KA-0058, KA-0902]
- [ ] **Check for Overage Charges:** Are there fees for exceeding baseline allowances? [KA-0067, KA-0106]
- [ ] **Verify Supply Caps:** Are there maximum volume limits on supply or capacity? [KA-0094, KA-0590]
- [ ] **Confirm No Restrictions:** Is there an explicit statement of no volume-based restrictions? [KA-0333, KA-0429]
