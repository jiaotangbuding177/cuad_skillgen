## Evidence-Based Review Rules

### 1. Revenue/Profit Sharing
*Objective: Identify explicit revenue sharing, royalty, profit sharing, or lack thereof.*

- **Explicit Revenue Sharing Clauses**: Look for language mandating the sharing of income, credits, losses, or deductions between parties.
  - *Evidence*: "All income and credits, and all losses and deductions shall be owned and shared..." [KA-0009]
  - *Evidence*: "Zynga will pay to WPT ten percent (10%) of the cumulative Net Revenue..." [KA-0040]
  - *Evidence*: "Royalties will be payable by ArTara on Net Sales of Product..." [KA-0053]
  - *Evidence*: "The parties shall share certain revenues related to purchases made by Customers..." [KA-0175]
  - *Evidence*: "plan_b shall pay LICENSOR a share of its revenues as set forth in APPENDIX 2..." [KA-0247]
  - *Evidence*: "License Consideration Revenue Share as specified in this CONTENT DISTRIBUTION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT..." [KA-0328]
  - *Evidence*: "PageMaster Corporation will pay Go Call... 5% of all airtime renewal revenue." [KA-0338]
  - *Evidence*: "The Royalty Fee will be... twenty percent (20%) of Gross Revenues..." [KA-0342]
  - *Evidence*: "Client is required to pay Excite a variable revenue share based on sales generated..." [KA-0358]
  - *Evidence*: "Distributor will make royalty payments to IMNTV based on a Structure as follows: 55% of subscriber revenue..." [KA-0364]
  - *Evidence*: "Licensee shall pay to Fox... a royalty in the following amounts..." [KA-0368]
  - *Evidence*: "Fox shall pay to Licensee... a royalty in the following amounts..." [KA-0369]
  - *Evidence*: "Licensee shall pay a royalty... percentage of its Gross Revenues..." [KA-0380]
  - *Evidence*: "HCI shall be entitled to all net revenue... split 50/50 between the parties." [KA-0421]

- **Tiered or Threshold-Based Royalties**: Identify clauses where royalty rates change based on sales volume or thresholds.
  - *Evidence*: "Annual Net Sales... Annual Royalty Rate Percent Net Sales $0 - $25,000,000..." [KA-0054]
  - *Evidence*: "In the event that Annual Net Sales... surpass certain thresholds, ArTara will make milestone payments..." [KA-0055]
  - *Evidence*: "Latest Sports Lines 45% Worldwide..." [KA-0248]

- **Government/Policy Rebates Sharing**: Check for sharing of external incentives.
  - *Evidence*: "The policy rebate, award, and preferential treatment shall be shared by both Parties... 50/50." [KA-0159]

- **Franchise Fees as Revenue Sharing**: Identify continuing fees based on gross sales.
  - *Evidence*: "You will pay us... 5% of their gross sales revenue... as a continuing fee." [KA-0117]
  - *Evidence*: "You will contribute... 3% of gross sales to a collective advertising fund..." [KA-0118]

- **Explicit Exclusions (No Sharing)**: Identify clauses stating no revenue/profit sharing exists.
  - *Evidence*: "The parties do not contemplate a sharing of profits... indicating no revenue or profit sharing arrangement exists." [KA-0164]
  - *Evidence*: "The contract does not contain any clauses requiring the parties to share revenue or profits..." [KA-0330]
  - *Evidence*: "Consideration for the license... is included in the Purchase Price... no ongoing revenue or profit sharing obligations." [KA-0354]
  - *Evidence*: "There is no payment or compensation contemplated under this Agreement..." [KA-0407]

### 2. Price Restrictions
*Objective: Identify restrictions on pricing, price changes, or explicit freedom to set prices.*

- **Explicit Price Freedom**: Look for clauses granting the right to set prices independently.
  - *Evidence*: "The submission of advertising information... will not affect your right to determine your own prices..." [KA-0119]
  - *Evidence*: "The contract... does not restrict the Distributor's ability to set the resale price..." [KA-0331]
  - *Evidence*: "The Company may elect to dispose of the Products at any price or for no consideration in its sole discretion..." [KA-0904]
  - *Evidence*: "XFN... shall have the right to charge users... no clauses restricting XFN's ability to set, raise, or reduce these prices." [KA-0924]

- **Price Change Restrictions (Caps/Notice)**: Identify limits on how often or how much prices can change.
  - *Evidence*: "VOTOCAST expressly reserves the right to change its rates... maximum of 10%." [KA-0900]
  - *Evidence*: "TrueLink agrees that the costs... will not increase for a period of one year..." [KA-0100]
  - *Evidence*: "Manufacturer shall have the right to make modifications... unless raw material costs vary significantly (>5%) and 60 days' notice is given..." [KA-0266]
  - *Evidence*: "Prices... shall only be subject to increase once per year... capping the increase at 5%, and requiring 90 days' notice." [KA-0361]
  - *Evidence*: "Erchonia may revise its prices... requiring 90 days' notice... restricted from increasing prices during the first 90 days..." [KA-0496]

- **Approval-Based Price Restrictions**: Identify requirements for counterparty approval before price changes.
  - *Evidence*: "Party B's pricing decisions are restricted by the requirement to obtain prior approval from Party A..." [KA-0440]
  - *Evidence*: "any changes to the prices... may be implemented only with formal confirmation from Party A." [KA-0441]
  - *Evidence*: "Party B is restricted from changing fee rates within three months... must obtain Party A's approval..." [KA-0442]
  - *Evidence*: "Products shall only be sold... at the retail price... as set forth... without mutual agreement from Calm..." [KA-0929]

- **Minimum/Maximum Price Floors/Ceils**: Identify fixed price constraints.
  - *Evidence*: "The Net Sales Price shall be commercially reasonable, and in no case shall the Net Sales Price be less than the material cost per unit." [KA-0587]
  - *Evidence*: "Prices shall include zero U.S. dollars input materials cost factor..." [KA-0588]
  - *Evidence*: "Reseller agrees not to publish or advertise any price below Manufacturer's Suggested List Price..." [KA-0613]
  - *Evidence*: "Designated Sponsor Products... may not be sold at a higher list price... compared to identical non-promotional products." [KA-0696]
  - *Evidence*: "Sponsor agrees that it will not increase the list price... of Designated Sponsor Products..." [KA-0697]

- **Price Protection/Adjustment**: Identify clauses adjusting price based on future transactions.
  - *Evidence*: "Global Energy shall issue additional common shares... such that Oxbow's adjusted per-share price... restricts the effective price..." [KA-0884]

- **Unilateral Price Change Rights**: Identify clauses allowing unrestricted price changes.
  - *Evidence*: "JRVS shall have the right to revise JRVS Price at any time... applying increases immediately upon notice." [KA-0483]

- **No Price Restrictions (Royalty-Free/No Payment)**: Identify contexts where price restrictions are irrelevant.
  - *Evidence*: "There is no payment or compensation contemplated... no prices to restrict..." [KA-0408]
  - *Evidence*: "Royalty-free license... does not involve the sale of goods... subject to price restrictions." [KA-0427]
  - *Evidence*: "Intellectual property assignment with a fixed nominal consideration... no clauses restricting price adjustments..." [KA-0528]
  - *Evidence*: "Royalty-free license... no price restrictions or fees..." [KA-0719]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not contain any clauses restricting the ability... to raise or reduce the prices..." [KA-0850]

### 3. Minimum Commitment
*Objective: Identify minimum financial commitments, purchase obligations, or explicit lack thereof.*

- **Minimum Financial Guarantees/Royalties**: Look for guaranteed payments regardless of performance.
  - *Evidence*: "Zynga will pay WPT three million U.S. dollars ($3,000,000) per year..." [KA-0041]
  - *Evidence*: "ArTara will provide thirty thousand dollars ($30,000) per year in funding..." [KA-0057]
  - *Evidence*: "Minimum Royalty means a minimum monthly Royalty Fee payment..." [KA-0344]
  - *Evidence*: "Licensee shall pay to Fox a non-refundable guarantee... regardless of sales performance." [KA-0371]
  - *Evidence*: "Minimum recoupable guarantee amounts allocated to individual properties..." [KA-0372]
  - *Evidence*: "HCI shall pay MLX a guaranteed fixed sum... per annum..." [KA-0424]
  - *Evidence*: "Party B will pay Party A RMB three thousand (3,000) as performance bond..." [KA-0444]
  - *Evidence*: "Minimum monthly royalty payment of $700.00..." [KA-0520]
  - *Evidence*: "Minimum monthly advertising spend commitment of $3,000 or 5% of gross revenues..." [KA-0521]
  - *Evidence*: "Spend a minimum of $10,000 on marketing during the Grand Opening Period." [KA-0522]
  - *Evidence*: "Cooperation fee... RMB200 million... constitutes a minimum financial commitment..." [KA-0551]

- **Minimum Purchase/Volume Commitments**: Look for obligations to buy specific quantities or values.
  - *Evidence*: "Party B will purchase all electricity generated from the project..." [KA-0161]
  - *Evidence*: "Purchase all generated electricity from the project..." [KA-0162]
  - *Evidence*: "Impresse agrees to purchase... Banners and Newsletters... totaling at least a specified amount..." [KA-0492]
  - *Evidence*: "Impresse agrees to purchase at least $[*] of such Banners and Newsletters in each calendar quarter..." [KA-0493]

- **Development/Performance Commitments**: Look for obligations to meet schedules or milestones.
  - *Evidence*: "Developer must submit one or more site(s)... opening of a specific number of restaurants each calendar year..." [KA-0293]
  - *Evidence*: "Failure to meet the minimum commitment of securing sites... constitutes a default..." [KA-0294]
  - *Evidence*: "Failure to open restaurants by the scheduled dates as an event of default." [KA-0295]

- **Explicit Exclusions (No Minimum Commitment)**: Identify clauses stating no minimums exist.
  - *Evidence*: "Joint Venturers shall not be required to make any capital contributions... unless mutually agreed otherwise." [KA-0011]
  - *Evidence*: "Producer agrees... it will receive no upfront compensation... no obligation to exercise its rights... no minimum commitment..." [KA-0329]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract requires... 'Yearly non-binding sales forecasts'... does not stipulate a minimum order quantity, minimum purchase amount, or minimum sales target..." [KA-0332]
  - *Evidence*: "Parties are working cooperatively... does not impose any minimum order sizes, purchase amounts, or mandatory unit commitments." [KA-0409]
  - *Evidence*: "Trademark license with no purchase obligations, minimum order sizes, or minimum usage commitments..." [KA-0428]
  - *Evidence*: "Explicitly states there is no direct sales requirement or minimum order commitment..." [KA-0433]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not involve the purchase of goods or services with minimum order quantities..." [KA-0529]

### 4. Volume Restriction
*Objective: Identify fee changes, caps, or restrictions based on usage volume or thresholds.*

- **Volume-Based Fee Increases (Tiered Royalties)**: Look for rates that increase as volume exceeds thresholds.
  - *Evidence*: "Royalty rate increases... as the volume of Net Sales exceeds specific thresholds ($25M and $50M)." [KA-0058]
  - *Evidence*: "Royalty rate increases as the number of subscribers (volume) exceeds specific thresholds..." [KA-0381]
  - *Evidence*: "Revenue share percentage changes (increases for Kubient) if the revenue exceeds the threshold." [KA-0535]
  - *Evidence*: "Fees increase based on the volume of monthly video views, with higher tiers costing more..." [KA-0902]
  - *Evidence*: "Royalty rates that change based on specific volume thresholds..." [KA-0470]
  - *Evidence*: "Specific fee increase (overage charge) if the bandwidth usage exceeds the 10 G threshold." [KA-0067]

- **Volume-Based Fee Decreases**: Look for rates that decrease as volume increases.
  - *Evidence*: "Fee rate decreases significantly after the cumulative generation exceeds 800 million KWH." [KA-0163]

- **Volume Caps/Limits**: Look for maximum limits on usage or supply.
  - *Evidence*: "Cap on the total purchase price (volume/value) of equipment per schedule..." [KA-0020]
  - *Evidence*: "Cap on the fulfillment fees... limiting the total cost to $10,000 per project regardless of volume." [KA-0032]
  - *Evidence*: "Company is not obligated to supply products exceeding 120% of the maximum quantity... effectively capping the volume..." [KA-0094]
  - *Evidence*: "Additional fees if BNL exceeds the initial allowance... effectively restricting volume without extra cost." [KA-0106]
  - *Evidence*: "Restriction on the volume of capacity Eutectix must reserve, capped at 10% of its manufacturing capacity." [KA-0590]

- **No Volume Restrictions**: Identify clauses stating no volume-based fees or limits exist.
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not impose any fees, consent requirements, or restrictions based on the volume of products purchased or sold..." [KA-0333]
  - *Evidence*: "No payment or compensation... no volume-based fees, thresholds, or restrictions on usage." [KA-0410]
  - *Evidence*: "License is royalty-free and does not contain any volume-based fees, thresholds, or restrictions..." [KA-0429]
  - *Evidence*: "No usage thresholds, volume-based fees, or consent requirements..." [KA-0530]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not contain any clauses imposing fee increases or consent requirements based on usage thresholds..." [KA-0581]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not impose fees, consent requirements, or restrictions based on the volume of product usage..." [KA-0852]
  - *Evidence*: "License is exclusive and the fee is fixed; there are no provisions for fee increases... based on the volume of distribution..." [KA-0926]
  - *Evidence*: "No penalties, fee increases, or consent requirements triggered by exceeding or failing to meet specific sales volume thresholds." [KA-0906]
  - *Evidence*: "Fees are calculated on a per-inquiry basis without stated caps or tiered penalties." [KA-0004]
  - *Evidence*: "No volume thresholds or fee increases specified... additional services are handled through good faith negotiation." [KA-0008]
  - *Evidence*: "Variable pricing based on destination but does not impose fees or consent requirements based on usage volume thresholds." [KA-0029]
  - *Evidence*: "Contract does not contain clauses imposing fee increases or consent requirements based on exceeding usage or volume thresholds." [KA-0084]
  - *Evidence*: "Supplier is obligated to meet forecasted needs, but there is no clause imposing fee increases or consent requirements if usage exceeds a certain threshold." [KA-0088]
